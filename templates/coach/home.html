What Poise measures

Pace

Syllables per second, compared against a comfortable 130-170 words-per-minute range.

Fluency

Pauses per minute and how much of your answer is actual speech versus silence.

Clarity

A pronunciation score estimated from syllable nuclei and voicing in your audio.

Expressiveness

Pitch variation - catches a flat, monotone delivery as well as an erratic one.

Body language

Pose estimation on your video: slouching, tilted head or shoulders, looking away and framing.
